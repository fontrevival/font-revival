# Bruce Fatface 1828 preparation

The Two Line Minion No. 1 setting supplies a complete capitals-only titling
alphabet, ligatures, figures and its printed punctuation. The source size remains
provenance; this is one scalable family. Its broader, heavier capitals distinguish
it from the Baltimore Bold, Two Line and Title revivals. The specimen date does
not claim a first issue or individual designer.

The retained native PNG was extracted directly from PDF page 121 of the
HathiTrust/NYPL copy mirrored by Circuitous Root. The reading image is an exact
270-degree counterclockwise rotation, without scaling. Acquisition hashes and
the container URL are in reference/acquisition.json.

Rows were visually transcribed and checked against the individual selected crops.
The dollar sign has a separate row rectangle so its ascender is retained without
including the Q tail above the zero. Recorded row baselines preserve the Q tail,
currency extents and punctuation levels. A 700-unit cap reference scales all
source characters uniformly; individual letters are not stretched. Sidebearings
and the blank space are new digital spacing. Lowercase input aliases capitals;
ASCII apostrophe aliases the observed closing apostrophe. No missing visible
characters were invented.

Potrace 1.16 fits the native masks after a 0.4-pixel blur and threshold 150.
Connected components smaller than four native pixels are discarded, and enclosed
white specks of at most three pixels are filled. This removes isolated scan dirt
while preserving thin serifs and punctuation components. Y retains only its
complete connected letter, discarding an isolated scan speck to the left. The exact recipes are
in tracing.json, with row measurements in rows.json.
