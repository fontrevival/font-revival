# Historical source preparation

Native JP2 leaves retain the source context. `crops.json` records exact native coordinates and lossless quarter-turns; `tracing.json` retains Potrace 1.16 thresholds, despeckling and fitted scale. Uniform scaling preserves engraved proportions. Adjacent sorts and caption fragments are excluded by reviewed crop boundaries and explicit erasures. `contour-cleanup.json` records removal of neighbouring fragments outside the medallion contour bounds. `drawings.json` preserves the final fitted outlines.

Distinct border corners and repeats are separate sorts. Full check strips preserve their terminals. Repeated settings, smaller impressions and compositions resolve to existing IDs in `inventory.json`, without making point-size offerings. Technical signs that repeat existing artwork remain provenance records. No missing content is reconstructed.
