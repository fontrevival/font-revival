# Bruce Shaded 1828 preparation

Two Line Brevier Shaded supplies the cleaner complete capitals and ligatures.
The matching Nonpareil Shaded setting supplies all ten figures, pound and dollar
signs and its four punctuation marks. Both share the outlined face and cast
shadow construction. Each is scaled using its measured cap reference.
All significant disconnected inner-letter parts are retained; native components
smaller than 100pixels remove isolated scan dirt. The pound sign instead retains
its single complete component to exclude the neighbouring Z fragment.

The native HathiTrust/NYPL PDF image is retained unchanged. A 270-degree
quarter turn and a measured 4.891-degree counterclockwise rotation correct
page tilt. The latter uses Pillow bicubic sampling; the full-page result and
angle measurements are retained. No nonuniform stretching is applied.

The exact source rectangles and settings are in rows.json and tracing.json.
Potrace 1.16 fits masks after a 0.3-pixel blur, threshold 135 and filling enclosed
white specks of at most one native pixel. Glyph height and baseline are measured
after component cleanup, preserving uniform source scale and original extents.

No visible characters are inferred. Lowercase input aliases the capitals, and
spaces and bearings are new digital spacing. The source-size labels document
the supplying impressions; this is one scalable font.
