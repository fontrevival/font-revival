# Historical source preparation

The complete capital alphabet, figures and accompanying symbols are directly
traced from the cited native Howe specimen. Lowercase input aliases the capitals;
no missing visible characters are invented. Spaces and sidebearings are digital.

The exact JP2, an unresampled quarter-turn and a measured rigid deskew are retained.
The angle and method are in `reference/derivation.json`. This corrects scan tilt
while retaining the intentional letter slant and shadow direction.

`rows.json` records the transcribed rows, and `tracing.json` records every native
crop and preparation setting. Uniform source scale is measured from flat capitals;
figures use their own row reference. Punctuation retains its source position
relative to the capital baseline. No nonuniform stretching is used.

Potrace 1.16 fits the documented masks after a 0.25-pixel blur and threshold 150.
Disconnected parts are retained according to each recipe. No white counters are
filled. Compare the historical and digital proof before changing these settings.

The C has a visibly interrupted outer arc in the source. This feature is retained
without inventing a bridge; the base letter and all other alphabet characters are
present. A later matching impression may resolve whether this is wear or the cut.
