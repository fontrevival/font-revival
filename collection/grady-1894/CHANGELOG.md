# Changes

- Offer fonts and editable font data under the choice MIT or SIL OFL 1.1,
  with both license notices in the fonts and download packages.
- Retain the existing outlines, character assignments, spacing and features.
  Standalone artwork and other original project files remain MIT licensed.

- Update copyright and release metadata to Harold Lehmann; retain the MIT license.
- Preserve glyph outlines, character coverage, spacing, and OpenType features.

## 1.000

- Initial Grady 1894 revival with printable Basic Latin and typographic punctuation.
- Retain historical references, rights evidence, measured preparation recipe, explicit reconstructions and editable CFF master.
- Include desktop/web releases, complete specimen and source-comparison proof.
